## More Languages! 🌍

Track & Graph now offers 67 language options!

I used AI to translate the app, so please keep an eye out for bad translations, and if you want to help out, please create a GitHub issue with anything that looks wrong in your preferred language 🙏

On Android 13 and newer, you can also choose **App language** from the navigation drawer to set Track & Graph's language independently of your phone's language.

---

## Improved Graph Legibility! 📊

All graphs are now implemented with custom canvas code. (R.I.P. AndroidPlot and Vico. Your services have been much appreciated 🙏)

X-axis labels now actually point at data points and have nicer, more legible date/time formatting. The labels also stick to the data and subdivide when you pan and zoom in full-screen mode.

---

## One Time Reminders! ⏰

You can now create a **One Time** reminder without linking it to a tracker or function. Choose a specific date and time, or a delay such as “in two hours”. You can also set a repeat interval for follow-up notifications after the first reminder.

**Time Since Last** reminders also have a new option: you can choose a specific time of day for the notification. For example, remind yourself at 9 am two days after your last entry.

---

## Bug Fixes and Improvements

- Improved keyboard focus and dialog resizing animations when moving between trackers while adding data points.
- Improved backup restoration, with checks for damaged or incompatible backup files before replacing your data.
- CSV export now asks where to save the file after you select what to export, avoiding empty files if you cancel before exporting.
- Fixed a bug where the import/export dialog sometimes failed to reopen after closing.
