## Reminders in Groups! ⏰

**Version 10.4.0** adds the ability to put reminders in groups. Reminder notifications take you straight to the group containing that reminder. 

---

## A New Add Component Flow! ✨

The add component flow also got a re-design. The old drop-down menu is now a proper dialog with icons for every component type. It also takes you straight into the setup flow when creating groups and reminders.
